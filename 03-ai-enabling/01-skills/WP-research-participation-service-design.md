# Service Design Specification: Client Research Participation

## Context

Clients often join user research sessions without understanding their role, leading to contaminated data, awkward dynamics, and missed learning opportunities. This service design maps the client's journey and designs interventions to transform them from "observer who contaminates" to "insight partner who accelerates discovery."

---

## Job-to-Be-Done

> "When my team is conducting user research, I want to participate productively without ruining the research, so I can gain deep customer understanding that informs better decisions."

---

## The Four Forces

### 1. Pushes (Pain of Current State)

| Push | Evidence | Impact |
|------|----------|--------|
| **Feel disconnected from research** | Clients pay for research but feel removed from insights | Lower buy-in, challenge findings later |
| **Don't trust secondhand insights** | "I need to see it myself to believe it" | Request to sit in without preparation |
| **FOMO on customer contact** | Rare chance to hear from actual users | Show up unprepared, react poorly |
| **Defend product/team in the moment** | Natural protective instinct when users criticize | Interrupt, explain away, justify decisions |
| **Don't know what "good" looks like** | No frame of reference for research behavior | Anxious, overcompensate, contaminate |

### 2. Pulls (Appeal of New Solution)

| Pull | Aspiration | Outcome |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Be the "client who gets it"** | Want consultant respect, not eye-rolls | Better partnership, deeper insights |
| **Gain confidence in decisions** | Research validates (or redirects) strategy | Make bolder moves with evidence |
| **Spot patterns consultants miss** | Internal context + user feedback = breakthrough | Unique insights from dual perspective |
| **Model customer-centricity** | Show team what "listening" looks like | Culture shift beyond this project |
| **Accelerate learning** | Direct observation beats reports | Faster decisions, less "can we validate that?" |

### 3. Anxieties (Fears Preventing Change)

| Anxiety | Root Fear | Manifestation |
|---------|-----------|---------------|
| **"I'll say something stupid"** | Loss of credibility with consultant | Stay silent, don't ask questions |
| **"I'll bias the research"** | Ruin expensive research investment | Freeze up, overly passive |
| **"Users will hate our product"** | Ego threat, team defense | Justify, explain, interrupt |
| **"I won't understand the process"** | Feel foolish asking basic questions | Pretend to understand, miss nuance |
| **"My presence changes behavior"** | Users will perform for the boss | Question validity of findings |

### 4. Habits (Behaviors That Override Judgment)

| Habit | Pattern | Why It Persists |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| **Defending the product** | Jump in to explain/justify when users criticize | Years of selling, pitching, protecting |
| **Solving in real-time** | "Have you tried...?" to users | Action bias, problem-solver identity |
| **Leading questions** | "Don't you think X would help?" | Want validation, not discovery |
| **Checking devices** | Phone out during "boring" parts | Used to multitasking, nervous energy |
| **Debriefing too soon** | "What did you think?" before patterns emerge | Impatient for answers, anxious about ambiguity |

## Current State Journey Map

### Stage 1: Pre-Session (1-24 Hours Before)

| Frontstage (Client Experience) | Backstage (Our Process) | Pain Points |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------|-------------|
| Receive calendar invite | Send invite with logistics only | No context on role, behavior, or what to expect |
| Feel excited + anxious | No proactive coaching | Client arrives unprepared, defensive |
| Wonder "Should I prepare?" | No preparation guidance | Unsure if they should review product, prep questions |
| Guess at their role | Assume they know research norms | Anxious, might not show up |

**Emotional State:** Excited opportunity → Rising anxiety → Confusion about expectations

### Stage 2: Session Start (First 15 Minutes)

| Frontstage | Backstage | Pain Points |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Enter room, unsure where to sit | No spatial guidance | Awkward positioning, too close/far from user |
| Wonder "Can I talk?" | No explicit role clarity | Freeze or over-participate |
| Watch moderator introduce | Hope to pick up cues | Miss user's body language watching moderator |
| Feel self-conscious | No reassurance | Hyper-aware of own presence, not user's experience |

**Emotional State:** Anxiety spike → Self-consciousness → Hyper-vigilance

### Stage 3: During Session (Active Research)

| Frontstage | Backstage | Pain Points |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Watch user struggle with product | Take notes (maybe) | Don't know what to capture, miss patterns |
| Hear criticism of their team's work | Maintain poker face | Internal defense, miss next 3 user comments |
| Want to explain/help user | Restrain impulse | Physical tension, cognitive load from restraint |
| Notice something moderator missed | Don't know if/how to intervene | Insight lost, frustration builds |
| See pattern from previous session | No framework to document | Pattern recognized but not captured |

**Emotional State:** Engagement → Defensiveness → Frustration → Lost opportunity awareness

### Stage 4: Session End (Last 10 Minutes)

| Frontstage | Backstage | Pain Points |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Want to ask user clarifying question | Moderator wraps | Missed chance to explore insight |
| Formulate "what we should have asked" | Thank user, close | Client epiphany comes too late |
| Uncertain if they can speak to user | No guidance on post-session protocol | Awkward lingering or abrupt exit |

**Emotional State:** Urgency → Regret → Uncertainty

### Stage 5: Debrief (Immediately After)

| Frontstage | Backstage | Pain Points |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Burst with reactions | Listen to hot takes | Jump to conclusions from n=1 |
| "Did you see when they...?" | Capture emotional reactions | Lose nuance, cement first impression |
| Want to solve immediately | Encourage patience for patterns | Frustration with "let's wait" response |
| Compare to own assumptions | Moderate overinterpretation | Confirmation bias, cherry-pick evidence |

**Emotional State:** Relief + Excitement → Impatience → Defensiveness if redirected

### Stage 6: Synthesis Participation (Days Later)

| Frontstage | Backstage | Pain Points |
|-----------|-----------|-------------|
| Review findings deck | Synthesize across all sessions | Client attended 2 of 10, missing context |
| "That's not what I saw" | Reconcile client memory vs. full data | Tension, question research validity |
| Feel disconnected from synthesis | Work product created without them | Lower ownership of findings |
| Wonder if presence mattered | No explicit role in synthesis | Passive consumer, not active partner |

**Emotional State:** Disconnection → Skepticism → Passivity

## Future State Journey Map

### Stage 1: Pre-Session Enablement

| Frontstage | Backstage | Touchpoint | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Receive Research Participation Guide (48hr before) | Automated skill delivery via email | **Skill activation: Pre-session brief** | Clear expectations, reduced anxiety |
| Read 3-min guide: role, do's/don'ts, what to observe | Curated micro-learning | **Skill content: Observer playbook** | Confident in role |
| Optional: 15-min coaching call | Calendar link in guide | **Human touchpoint: Coach call** | Address specific anxieties |
| Receive note-taking template | Template attached to guide | **Tool: Observation worksheet** | Know what to capture |

**Transformation:** Anxious → Prepared → Confident

### Stage 2: Session Start Ritual

| Frontstage | Backstage | Touchpoint | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Moderator names client role explicitly | Scripted introduction | **Facilitation: Role clarity** | Permission to observe |
| "You're here to observe and learn. I'll manage the conversation. Take notes on [specific prompts]." | 30-second framing | **Verbal contract** | Anxiety relieved |
| Client positioned out of user's direct sightline | Spatial setup | **Physical environment** | Less self-conscious |
| Receive silent cue card with reminders | Physical prop | **Ambient reminder: Behavior prompts** | Anchor when tempted to speak |

**Transformation:** Self-conscious → Clear role → Present

### Stage 3: During Session Support

| Frontstage | Backstage | Touchpoint | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Use note-taking template with prompts | Structured capture tool | **Skill tool: Observation framework** | Focused attention |
| Notice defense impulse, breathe, use cue card | Self-regulation technique | **Internal practice** | Restraint without tension |
| Moderator gives "Observer question?" pause (if appropriate) | Facilitation technique | **Invitation to participate** | Safe way to contribute |
| Mark "🔥" on template for follow-up insights | Symbol system | **Capture mechanism** | Insights preserved without interruption |

**Transformation:** Reactive → Observant → Strategic contributor

### Stage 4: Session End Protocol

| Frontstage | Backstage | Touchpoint | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Moderator: "Before we close, [Client], any clarifying question?" | Explicit invitation | **Facilitation: Client question window** | Safe contribution point |
| Client asks prepared question from template | Framework-guided inquiry | **Skill application** | Quality question, not defensive reaction |
| Post-session: "Great question timing" feedback | Positive reinforcement | **Coaching moment** | Behavior reinforcement |

**Transformation:** Regretful → Satisfied → Capable

### Stage 5: Structured Debrief

| Frontstage | Backstage | Touchpoint | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Moderator facilitates 10-min structured debrief | Debrief protocol | **Skill activation: Debrief framework** | Channel energy productively |
| "What surprised you? What confirmed? What contradicted?" | Three-question framework | **Facilitation structure** | Nuanced reflection vs. hot takes |
| Co-create session summary in shared doc | Live documentation | **Collaborative artifact** | Shared understanding |
| "Pattern watch": Track across sessions on Miro board | Visual synthesis tool | **Cumulative learning tool** | See patterns emerge |

**Transformation:** Reactive → Reflective → Pattern-aware

### Stage 6: Synthesis Partnership

| Frontstage | Backstage | Touchpoint | Outcome |
|-----------|-----------|------------|---------|
| Receive "Your role in synthesis" guide | Follow-up skill delivery | **Skill activation: Synthesis participation** | Clear next step |
| Join synthesis working session | Facilitated collaboration | **Workshop: Co-synthesis** | Active partner, not passive consumer |
| Contribute context: "In that moment, they were reacting to [internal system we know is broken]" | Client insider knowledge | **Unique contribution** | Valuable dual perspective |
| Review findings with "challenge" permission | Explicit invitation to dissent | **Validation ritual** | Trust in process + ownership |

**Transformation:** Passive consumer → Active partner → Insight co-creator

## Struggling Moments (Prioritized)

| Moment | Frequency | Severity | Root Cause | Intervention |
|--------|-----------|----------|------------|--------------|
| **Client defends product during session** | 60% of sessions | Critical | Ego threat + no behavior guidance | Pre-session coaching + in-room cue card |
| **Client jumps to conclusions from n=1** | 80% of sessions | High | No framework for pattern recognition | Debrief structure + pattern tracking tool |
| **Client feels useless/passive** | 50% of sessions | Medium | Unclear role + no contribution mechanism | Explicit role framing + observation template |
| **Client asks leading questions** | 30% of sessions | High | Sales/pitch habit + no question training | Question framework + moderator gating |
| **Client disconnects from synthesis** | 70% of engagements | Medium | Excluded from process + memory fades | Synthesis participation guide + workshop invitation |

## Implementation Implications

### Architecture

- **Skill delivery system** — Automated guide distribution tied to calendar invites
- **Template library** — Standardized observation worksheets, debrief frameworks
- **Pattern tracking tool** — Miro board or similar for cumulative session tracking
- **Learning management** — Track which clients have received guides, completed prep

### Integration Points

- **Calendar system** — Trigger guide send 48 hours before session
- **Research Copilot** — Integrate client observations into synthesis
- **Session recordings** — Timestamp client "🔥" moments for review
- **CRM** — Track client research participation patterns

### Data Flows

1. Calendar event created → Trigger guide delivery
2. Client opens guide → Log completion, offer coaching call
3. Session occurs → Capture client notes + timestamps
4. Debrief happens → Log insights in research system
5. Synthesis workshop → Merge client context with research patterns

### Performance Requirements

- **Guide delivery** — <5 minutes after calendar event created
- **Template load** — <2 seconds in session
- **Pattern board updates** — Real-time during debrief
- **Synthesis invite** — Within 24 hours of final session

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Client receives Research Participation Guide 48 hours before first session
- [ ] Guide reading time ≤3 minutes with clear role definition
- [ ] Observation template provided with specific prompts aligned to research goals
- [ ] Moderator explicitly frames client role at session start (30-second script)
- [ ] Client has mechanism to capture insights without interrupting (note template)
- [ ] Structured debrief occurs within 10 minutes post-session
- [ ] Client invited to synthesis workshop with clear participation guide
- [ ] Client confidence rating >8/10 post-engagement
- [ ] Reduction in session interruptions by >50%
- [ ] Client contributions captured and visible in final research deliverable

---

## Open Questions for Technical Architect

1. **Automation feasibility** — Can we auto-trigger guide delivery from calendar events, or does this need manual initiation?
2. **Template format** — What's the best format for note-taking templates? Google Doc, PDF, Notion, printed handout?
3. **Pattern board integration** — Should Miro board be real-time collaborative, or async after each session?
4. **Skill versioning** — How do we customize guides for different research types (interviews vs. usability tests vs. field observations)?
5. **Data capture** — Where do client observations get stored for synthesis integration? Research Copilot database?
6. **Metrics tracking** — What's the lightest way to measure client confidence, interruption rates, contribution counts?
7. **Scaling** — If client brings 3 team members to session, does each get individual guide or one shared briefing?
