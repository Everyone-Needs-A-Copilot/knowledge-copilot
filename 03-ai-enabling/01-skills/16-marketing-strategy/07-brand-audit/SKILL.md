# Brand Audit

A brand is not your logo or your color palette. It is the gut feeling people have when they encounter you. A brand audit is the process of discovering whether that gut feeling is the one you intended. It is the gap analysis between who you say you are and how people actually experience you.

Most brands have consistency problems they cannot see because they are too close. The website says one thing, the social media feels different, the sales team pitches something else, and customer support sounds like a completely different company. A brand audit shines a light on every crack and gives you a plan to fix them.

---

## Framework

### Brand Audit Framework: Five Dimensions

Audit each dimension independently, then assess the whole picture.

| Dimension | What You Are Assessing | Key Question |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------|
| **Voice** | Tone, language, personality across all content | Does every touchpoint sound like the same person? |
| **Visual** | Logo, color, typography, imagery, layout | Does every touchpoint look like the same brand? |
| **Positioning** | Market position, differentiation, value prop | Do we describe ourselves the way our audience describes us? |
| **Experience** | Customer journey, interactions, service quality | Does the experience match the promise? |
| **Perception** | How audience/market actually sees the brand | Is the perception aligned with our intent? |

### Voice Consistency Audit

#### Voice Attribute Definition

Define your brand voice as 3-4 attributes with a spectrum:

| Attribute | We Are | We Are Not |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| Example: Confident | Direct, assured, backs claims with evidence | Arrogant, dismissive, preachy |
| Example: Approachable | Conversational, relatable, uses plain language | Slangy, unprofessional, overly casual |
| Example: Sharp | Concise, insightful, cuts through noise | Blunt, cold, lacking empathy |

### Positioning Analysis

#### Internal vs External Perception

| Dimension | How We Describe Ourselves | How Audience Describes Us | Gap |
|-----------|--------------------------|--------------------------|-----|
| **What we do** | | | |
| **Who we serve** | | | |
| **What makes us different** | | | |
| **Why choose us** | | | |
| **Our personality** | | | |

**Data sources for external perception:**
- Customer interviews (5-10 conversations minimum)
- Online reviews and testimonials (extract language patterns)
- Social media mentions (how people talk about you)
- Sales call recordings (what prospects say before buying)
- Support tickets (how customers describe their experience)
- NPS/survey verbatims (open-ended responses)

## Define Done

- [ ] Strategy grounded in audience data and market context
- [ ] Channels and tactics aligned to audience behavior
- [ ] Success metrics defined with specific targets
- [ ] Budget and resource requirements estimated

## Application

### Brand Audit Scoring Rubric

#### Dimension Scoring

| Score | Label | Description |
|-------|-------|------------|
| **5** | Excellent | Fully consistent, intentional, aligned with strategy |
| **4** | Good | Mostly consistent, minor deviations |
| **3** | Adequate | Some consistency, noticeable gaps |
| **2** | Poor | Inconsistent, multiple issues |
| **1** | Critical | No consistency, contradictory signals |

#### Overall Brand Health Score

| Dimension | Weight | Score | Weighted Score |
|-----------|--------|-------|---------------|
| Voice consistency | 25% | _/5 | _/1.25 |
| Visual consistency | 20% | _/5 | _/1.00 |
| Positioning clarity | 25% | _/5 | _/1.25 |
| Experience alignment | 20% | _/5 | _/1.00 |
| Perception accuracy | 10% | _/5 | _/0.50 |
| **Total** | **100%** | | **_/5.00** |

**Interpretation:**

| Score Range | Brand Health | Priority |
|-------------|-------------|----------|
| 4.5-5.0 | Exceptional | Maintain and refine |
| 3.5-4.4 | Healthy | Address specific weak areas |
| 2.5-3.4 | Needs work | Focused improvement plan |
| Below 2.5 | Urgent attention | Comprehensive brand refresh |

### Gap Identification Methodology

For each gap found, document:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| **Gap description** | What is inconsistent or misaligned |
| **Touchpoints affected** | Where the gap appears |
| **Severity** | Critical / High / Medium / Low |
| **Root cause** | Why the gap exists (missing guidelines, multiple creators, outdated assets, etc.) |
| **Recommended fix** | Specific action to close the gap |
| **Owner** | Who is responsible for the fix |
| **Dependencies** | What else needs to change first |

### Recommendations Framework

Prioritize brand audit findings using this matrix:

| | Low Effort | High Effort |
|---|-----------|------------|
| **High Impact** | Quick wins: do these first | Strategic projects: plan and execute |
| **Low Impact** | Fill-in work: do when capacity allows | Deprioritize: revisit next audit |

## Quick Reference

### Brand Audit Process (Step by Step)

1. Define brand voice attributes (3-4 attributes with "we are / we are not")
2. Inventory all touchpoints across the customer journey
3. Collect samples from every touchpoint (screenshots, recordings, documents)
4. Score voice consistency across all touchpoints
5. Score visual consistency across all channels
6. Conduct positioning analysis (internal vs external perception)
7. Map experience alignment (promise vs reality)
8. Gather perception data (interviews, reviews, mentions)
9. Calculate overall brand health score
10. Identify gaps, prioritize by impact and effort
11. Create recommendations with owners and dependencies
12. Schedule next audit (recommended: every 6 months)

### Red Flags That Trigger an Immediate Audit

| Red Flag | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------------|
| Customer says "I didn't know you did that" about a core offering | Positioning gap |
| Team members describe the brand differently | Internal alignment failure |
| New hire content sounds nothing like existing content | Voice documentation gap |
| Rebrand or merger completed in last 12 months | Transition consistency risk |
| Multiple agencies/freelancers producing content | Multi-voice risk |
| Customer complaints about "confusing" experience | Experience-brand disconnect |

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | What to Do Instead |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Auditing only visual identity** | Brand is far more than logos and colors | Audit all five dimensions: voice, visual, positioning, experience, perception |
| **Skipping external perception** | You cannot assess brand health from inside | Talk to real customers, read real reviews |
| **Audit without action plan** | Knowing the problems without fixing them is worse than not knowing | Every finding needs an owner, a fix, and a deadline |
| **One-time audit** | Brand consistency drifts over time, especially as teams grow | Audit every 6 months minimum |
| **Auditing in isolation** | Brand exists in customer context, not in a vacuum | Include customer journey and competitor context |
| **Perfectionism** | Waiting for perfect consistency before launching anything | Fix the critical gaps first, iterate on the rest |
| **Ignoring internal touchpoints** | Job postings, internal docs, and partner materials shape brand too | Include every touchpoint, not just customer-facing ones |
| **No voice documentation** | Without written guidelines, consistency is impossible to maintain | Document voice attributes before auditing voice consistency |


## Quality Tests

### Audience Alignment Test
- Strategy targets where the audience actually is, not assumptions
- **PASS:** Channel selection backed by audience research or data
- **FAIL:** Channels chosen based on trends rather than audience behavior

### Measurability Test
- Every tactic has a specific, measurable success metric
- **PASS:** KPIs defined with target numbers and measurement method
- **FAIL:** Goals are vague ('increase awareness') without measurable targets
