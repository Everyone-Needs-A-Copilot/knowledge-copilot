# Social Media Strategy

Social media strategy is not about being everywhere. It is about being unmissable somewhere. The brands that win on social are the ones that pick the right platforms, commit to a point of view, and build genuine community rather than chasing follower counts. A thousand engaged followers who share your content beat a hundred thousand passive ones who scroll past.

Stop thinking of social media as a broadcast channel. Start thinking of it as a room full of people you want to have a conversation with. Your strategy should answer: Which room? What conversation? And why would anyone want to talk to you?

## Framework

### Platform Selection Matrix

Do not choose platforms based on where everyone is. Choose based on where your specific audience is and what format plays to your strengths.

| Factor | Weight | Assessment Questions |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| **Audience presence** | 30% | Is your target persona active here? What do they use this platform for? |
| **Format alignment** | 25% | Does the platform's primary format match your content strengths? |
| **Competitive density** | 15% | How crowded is your niche on this platform? |
| **Resource requirement** | 15% | Can your team produce content at the quality and cadence this platform demands? |
| **Business objective fit** | 15% | Does this platform support your primary goal (awareness, leads, community, sales)? |

**Score each platform 1-5 per factor, multiply by weight. Prioritize the top 2-3.**

### Platform Strategy Templates

#### LinkedIn: Thought Leadership

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Primary goal** | Authority building, professional trust |
| **Content mix** | 40% insights/opinion, 30% how-to, 20% stories, 10% company |
| **Format priority** | Text posts (high reach), carousels (saves), articles (depth) |
| **Posting cadence** | 3-5x per week |
| **Engagement style** | Comment on industry conversations, reply to every comment on your posts |
| **Key metric** | Engagement rate, profile views, inbound connection requests |
| **Differentiation lever** | Personal voice from leadership, data-backed takes, practitioner stories |

#### Instagram: Visual Storytelling

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Primary goal** | Brand awareness, visual identity, community |
| **Content mix** | 30% educational carousels, 25% Reels, 25% Stories, 20% feed posts |
| **Format priority** | Reels (reach), carousels (saves/shares), Stories (connection) |
| **Posting cadence** | 4-7x per week (including Stories daily) |
| **Engagement style** | Story polls/questions, reply to DMs, comment engagement |
| **Key metric** | Saves, shares, Reel views, Story reply rate |
| **Differentiation lever** | Consistent visual identity, behind-the-scenes access, aesthetic differentiation |

#### Twitter/X: Conversation and Speed

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Primary goal** | Thought leadership, real-time engagement, network building |
| **Content mix** | 40% insights/takes, 25% threads, 20% replies/engagement, 15% curated |
| **Format priority** | Tweets (reach), threads (depth), quote tweets (network) |
| **Posting cadence** | 1-3x per day |
| **Engagement style** | Jump into relevant conversations, quote-tweet with adds, reply generously |
| **Key metric** | Impressions, engagement rate, follower quality, replies received |
| **Differentiation lever** | Speed of response, strong point of view, personality |

#### YouTube: Education and Discovery

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Primary goal** | Evergreen education, search-driven discovery |
| **Content mix** | 50% how-to/tutorials, 30% deep-dives, 20% opinion/trend |
| **Format priority** | Long-form (search), Shorts (discovery), playlists (binge) |
| **Posting cadence** | 1-2 long-form per week, 3-5 Shorts |
| **Engagement style** | Pin thoughtful comments, reply to questions, community tab polls |
| **Key metric** | Watch time, subscriber growth, search impressions |
| **Differentiation lever** | Production quality, teaching clarity, personality on camera |

#### TikTok: Discovery and Virality

| Element | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| **Primary goal** | Reach new audiences, brand discovery |
| **Content mix** | 40% educational, 30% entertaining, 20% trending, 10% promotional |
| **Format priority** | Short-form video (15-60 seconds sweet spot) |
| **Posting cadence** | 5-7x per week (algorithm rewards volume) |
| **Engagement style** | Reply to comments with video, duets, stitch trending content |
| **Key metric** | Views, completion rate, shares, follower conversion |
| **Differentiation lever** | Authenticity over polish, niche expertise, hook strength |

### Community Building vs Broadcasting

| Dimension | Broadcasting | Community Building |
|-----------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Mindset** | "Look at what we made" | "Let's figure this out together" |
| **Content** | Polished announcements | Conversations, questions, shared learning |
| **Engagement** | Post and move on | Respond, reference, remember |
| **Metrics** | Impressions, reach | Replies, DMs, repeat engagers |
| **Growth** | Follower count | Relationship depth |
| **Sustainability** | Dies when ad budget stops | Self-sustaining through word of mouth |

## Application

### Social Media Audit Template

Run this audit on each active platform quarterly.

| Audit Area | Questions to Answer | Current State | Action |
|-----------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| **Profile** | Bio clear? Links current? Visual identity consistent? | | |
| **Content mix** | Does actual mix match intended strategy? | | |
| **Cadence** | Are you hitting target posting frequency? | | |
| **Engagement rate** | Is it stable, growing, or declining? | | |
| **Audience growth** | Quality followers or vanity numbers? | | |
| **Top-performing content** | What resonated most? Why? | | |
| **Worst-performing content** | What flopped? Pattern? | | |
| **Competitor activity** | What are competitors doing well? Differently? | | |
| **Resource reality** | Is the team able to sustain this platform? | | |
| **ROI justification** | Is this platform delivering business value? | | |

### Content Repurposing Workflow

Maximize output from a single content investment:

| Source Content | Platform Adaptation | Format Change |
|---------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Blog post | LinkedIn text post | Key insight as a standalone take |
| Blog post | Twitter/X thread | Break into 5-8 tweet thread |
| Blog post | Instagram carousel | Visual summary of key points |
| Podcast episode | YouTube video | Full episode or highlight clips |
| Podcast episode | TikTok/Reels | 30-60 second key moment clips |
| Webinar | LinkedIn article | Written recap with slides |
| Customer story | Instagram Story | Behind-the-scenes narrative |
| Data report | Twitter/X | Single stat with commentary |

**Repurposing rule:** Adapt for the platform, do not just cross-post. Each platform has its own language.

### Platform Exit Criteria

Know when to leave a platform:

| Signal | Threshold | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Engagement declining steadily | 3+ months of decline with consistent effort | Investigate, then decide |
| Audience mismatch | Less than 20% of followers match target persona | Evaluate if worth continuing |
| Resource drain | Platform consuming more than 40% of social budget for less than 20% of results | Reduce investment or exit |
| Algorithm hostility | Platform deprioritizing your content format | Adapt format or redirect resources |
| Team burnout | Content quality visibly declining | Reduce cadence or exit |

## Quick Reference

### Social Media Strategy in 8 Steps

1. Define primary business objective for social (awareness, leads, community, sales)
2. Score and select top 2-3 platforms using the selection matrix
3. Build platform-specific strategy using the templates above
4. Set content mix percentages and cadence per platform
5. Create 2 weeks of content before launching
6. Focus first 90 days on posting consistency and engagement habits
7. Run platform audit at 90 days
8. Optimize or exit based on audit results

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | What to Do Instead |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Being on every platform** | Mediocre everywhere, excellent nowhere | Pick 2-3 and dominate |
| **Cross-posting identical content** | Each platform has different norms and formats | Adapt content for each platform |
| **Follower count obsession** | 50K followers who never engage are worthless | Focus on engagement rate and audience quality |
| **Posting without engaging** | Social is a conversation, not a bulletin board | Spend as much time engaging as posting |
| **Chasing every trend** | Trend-hopping without brand connection feels desperate | Only join trends that align with your pillars |
| **Ignoring DMs and comments** | You are telling your community they do not matter | Respond to every comment and DM within 24 hours |
| **No platform-specific strategy** | What works on LinkedIn fails on TikTok | Build a distinct strategy per platform |
| **Measuring only vanity metrics** | Impressions and followers do not pay bills | Track metrics tied to business outcomes |


## Quality Tests

### Audience Alignment Test
- Strategy targets where the audience actually is, not assumptions
- **PASS:** Channel selection backed by audience research or data
- **FAIL:** Channels chosen based on trends rather than audience behavior

### Measurability Test
- Every tactic has a specific, measurable success metric
- **PASS:** KPIs defined with target numbers and measurement method
- **FAIL:** Goals are vague ('increase awareness') without measurable targets
