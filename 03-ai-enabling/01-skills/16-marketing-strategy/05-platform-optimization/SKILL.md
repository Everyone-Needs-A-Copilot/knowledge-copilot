# Platform Optimization

Understanding how platforms work is not about gaming algorithms. It is about removing friction between your content and the people who need to see it. Algorithms are recommendation engines: they are trying to match content with interested users. Your job is to make that match as easy as possible by creating content the algorithm can understand, in formats it rewards, at times your audience is actually online.

Optimization without substance is manipulation. Substance without optimization is invisibility. You need both.

---

## Framework

### Algorithm Awareness Matrix

Each platform's algorithm rewards different signals. Understand what drives distribution on each.

| Platform | Primary Signal | Secondary Signals | Content Killer |
|----------|---------------|-------------------|---------------|
| **LinkedIn** | Dwell time (time reading) | Comments, especially long ones; reactions; shares | External links in post body (suppresses reach) |
| **Instagram (Feed)** | Relationship + interest | Saves, shares, comments, profile visits | Low-quality images, inconsistent posting |
| **Instagram (Reels)** | Completion rate + replays | Shares, saves, comments | Watermarks from other platforms, low resolution |
| **Twitter/X** | Engagement velocity (first 30 min) | Replies, retweets, bookmarks | No engagement triggers, posting into dead hours |
| **YouTube (Long)** | Watch time + session time | Click-through rate, likes, comments | Misleading thumbnails (high CTR but low watch time) |
| **YouTube (Shorts)** | Completion rate + replays | Likes, shares, subscribe clicks | Slow starts, no hook in first second |
| **TikTok** | Completion rate + replays | Shares, comments, follows from video | Slow hook, too long without payoff |

### Posting Time Optimization

#### General Best Practices by Platform

| Platform | Best Days | Peak Hours (audience local time) | Worst Times |
|----------|-----------|--------------------------------|-------------|
| LinkedIn | Tuesday-Thursday | 7-8 AM, 12 PM, 5-6 PM | Weekends, late night |
| Instagram | Monday-Friday | 11 AM-1 PM, 7-9 PM | 3-5 AM |
| Twitter/X | Monday-Friday | 8-10 AM, 12-1 PM | Late night weekdays |
| TikTok | Tuesday-Thursday | 6-9 PM, weekends 10 AM-12 PM | Early weekday mornings |
| YouTube | Thursday-Saturday | 2-4 PM (publish for evening viewing) | Monday mornings |
| Facebook | Wednesday-Friday | 9-11 AM, 1-3 PM | Early mornings, late night |

**Critical caveat:** These are averages. Your audience is not average. Use platform analytics to find your specific audience's active hours.

#### Audience Activity Analysis Process

1. Pull platform analytics for the last 90 days
2. Identify top 20% of posts by engagement rate
3. Map those posts to day and time published
4. Look for patterns (cluster analysis, not single data points)
5. Test your hypothesis for 4 weeks with consistent timing
6. Compare results to baseline
7. Adjust and repeat quarterly

## Application

### Hook Optimization

The first 3 seconds of video or first line of text determines whether anyone consumes your content.

#### Video Hook Formulas

| Hook Type | Structure | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Bold claim** | "[Surprising statement] - here's why" | "Your content strategy is backwards - here's why" |
| **Question** | "Have you ever [relatable frustration]?" | "Have you ever posted something you loved and got zero engagement?" |
| **Pattern interrupt** | Start with unexpected visual or statement | Jump cut, unusual camera angle, "Stop scrolling" |
| **Promise** | "By the end of this, you'll know [outcome]" | "By the end of this, you'll know exactly when to post" |
| **Contrast** | "Most people do X. Top performers do Y." | "Most brands post daily. Top brands post 3x a week." |

#### Text Hook Formulas

| Hook Type | Structure | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| **Contrarian take** | "Unpopular opinion: [statement]" | "Unpopular opinion: You don't need a content calendar" |
| **Specific result** | "[Specific outcome] with [specific method]" | "We grew newsletter subscribers by 340% with one change" |
| **Admission** | "I was wrong about [common belief]" | "I was wrong about posting every day" |
| **List tease** | "[Number] [things] most [audience] get wrong" | "5 things most marketers get wrong about LinkedIn" |
| **Story opening** | "Last [time period], [something happened]" | "Last quarter, our best-performing post was an accident" |

### Hashtag Strategy by Platform

| Platform | Strategy | Count | Placement |
|----------|----------|-------|-----------|
| **LinkedIn** | Niche + industry, no trending chasers | 3-5 | End of post or first comment |
| **Instagram** | Mix of niche (small), mid-size, and broad | 5-15 | Caption or first comment |
| **Twitter/X** | Only when joining a conversation or trending topic | 1-2 | Integrated into text |
| **TikTok** | Niche + trending sounds/effects matter more | 3-5 | Caption |
| **YouTube** | Tags matter less than title and description keywords | 5-10 in tags | Tags field + natural in description |

### Content Format Testing Methodology

Test format effectiveness systematically, not randomly.

| Step | Action | Duration |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Identify 3 format hypotheses to test | Planning phase |
| 2 | Create 4 pieces per format (minimum sample) | Production phase |
| 3 | Publish on same days/times for fair comparison | 4-week test |
| 4 | Measure engagement rate (not total engagement) | After all published |
| 5 | Identify winning format | Analysis |
| 6 | Increase winning format to 50% of content mix | Next 4 weeks |
| 7 | Continue testing runner-up formats at lower volume | Ongoing |

### Algorithm-Friendly vs Algorithm-Gaming

| Optimization (Do This) | Gaming (Do Not Do This) |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| Write strong hooks because they serve the reader | Write clickbait that misleads to get clicks |
| Post when audience is active for their benefit | Spam-post to flood the feed |
| Use native formats because they perform better | Repost without adapting to avoid effort |
| Encourage genuine comments with discussion prompts | Run engagement pods or buy fake comments |
| Optimize video length for the value delivered | Pad videos to hit an arbitrary length target |
| Use relevant hashtags for discoverability | Stuff unrelated trending hashtags |

## Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | What to Do Instead |
|-------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Optimizing before substance** | A perfectly optimized empty post is still empty | Nail the message first, then optimize delivery |
| **Copy-paste cross-posting** | Each platform has different norms, formats, and audiences | Adapt content for each platform specifically |
| **Ignoring platform analytics** | Generic best practices are averages, your audience is specific | Use your own data to find your audience's patterns |
| **Chasing algorithm changes weekly** | Constant pivoting kills consistency and brand identity | Follow major shifts, ignore weekly fluctuations |
| **Engagement pod participation** | Algorithms detect artificial engagement, penalizes over time | Build real engagement through quality content |
| **Over-hashtagging** | Looks spammy, dilutes discoverability | Use platform-appropriate counts of relevant tags |
| **Posting at "best times" from generic articles** | Your audience may be in different timezones or have different habits | Analyze your specific audience activity data |
| **Never testing formats** | Sticking with one format means missing what might work better | Run structured format tests quarterly |


## Quality Tests

### Audience Alignment Test
- Strategy targets where the audience actually is, not assumptions
- **PASS:** Channel selection backed by audience research or data
- **FAIL:** Channels chosen based on trends rather than audience behavior

### Measurability Test
- Every tactic has a specific, measurable success metric
- **PASS:** KPIs defined with target numbers and measurement method
- **FAIL:** Goals are vague ('increase awareness') without measurable targets
