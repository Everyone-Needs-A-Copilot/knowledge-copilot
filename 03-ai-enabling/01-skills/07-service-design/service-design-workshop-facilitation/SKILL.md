# Service Design Workshop Facilitation

## Overview

Plan and execute collaborative workshops that bring together stakeholders to explore, define, and design service experiences through structured activities and facilitated dialogue.


## Define Done

- [ ] Service experience mapped from the customer's perspective
- [ ] Touchpoints across all channels identified and evaluated
- [ ] Pain points prioritized by impact and frequency
- [ ] Improvement opportunities linked to business outcomes

## Instructions

### Step 1: Define Workshop Purpose

Clarify objectives and constraints:

| Element | Question | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| **Objective** | What decision or output is needed? | Align on customer journey priorities |
| **Participants** | Who needs to be in the room? | Product, support, sales, 2 customers |
| **Duration** | How much time is available? | Half-day (4 hours) |
| **Format** | In-person, remote, or hybrid? | Remote via Miro/Zoom |
| **Deliverable** | What tangible output? | Prioritized opportunity backlog |

### Step 2: Design Workshop Flow

Structure the session using the diverge-emerge-converge pattern:

| Phase | Purpose | Timing | Activities |
|-------|---------|--------|------------|
| **Opening** | Set context, build safety | 10% | Icebreaker, objectives, ground rules |
| **Diverge** | Generate breadth | 30% | Brainstorming, research share-out |
| **Emerge** | Find patterns | 30% | Clustering, voting, discussion |
| **Converge** | Make decisions | 20% | Prioritization, commitment |
| **Closing** | Lock in action | 10% | Next steps, feedback, appreciation |

### Step 3: Select Activities

Match activities to objectives:

| Objective | Recommended Activities |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Share research** | Research wall, empathy map, persona review |
| **Map current state** | Journey mapping, service blueprint, ecosystem map |
| **Identify opportunities** | How Might We, opportunity canvas, pain/gain mapping |
| **Generate ideas** | Crazy 8s, brainwriting, SCAMPER |
| **Prioritize** | Dot voting, 2x2 matrix, effort/impact grid |
| **Align on action** | Commitment canvas, RACI, sprint planning |

### Step 4: Prepare Materials

Gather everything needed:

| Category | In-Person | Remote |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Templates** | Printed canvases, large paper | Miro/FigJam boards |
| **Supplies** | Sticky notes, markers, dots | Digital stickies, stamps |
| **Technology** | Timer, projector | Zoom, breakout rooms |
| **Pre-work** | Printed research, personas | Shared docs, recordings |
| **Documentation** | Camera, recorder | Screen recording, notes |

### Step 5: Facilitate Effectively

Apply facilitation best practices:

| Technique | When to Use | How to Apply |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|
| **Silent brainstorm** | Prevent groupthink | Write before sharing |
| **Round robin** | Ensure all voices | Each person shares in turn |
| **Timeboxing** | Maintain momentum | Strict timers, visible countdown |
| **Parking lot** | Handle tangents | Capture, acknowledge, move on |
| **Temperature check** | Gauge energy | Quick polls, thumb voting |
| **Synthesis pause** | Create meaning | Reflect and summarize key points |

### Step 6: Document and Follow Up

Capture value and maintain momentum:

| Output | Timing | Owner |
|--------|--------|-------|
| **Photo documentation** | During workshop | Facilitator |
| **Raw notes** | Within 24 hours | Facilitator |
| **Synthesized summary** | Within 48 hours | Facilitator |
| **Action item tracking** | Within 1 week | Project lead |
| **Follow-up session** | Within 2 weeks | Facilitator |

## Quality Tests

### Customer Perspective Test
- Analysis reflects actual customer experience, not internal assumptions
- **PASS:** Insights are based on customer data, quotes, or observed behavior
- **FAIL:** Journey map reflects what we think customers experience, not reality

### Systemic Test
- Analysis considers the full service ecosystem, not just one touchpoint
- **PASS:** Cross-channel handoffs and backstage processes are included
- **FAIL:** Only the digital touchpoint analyzed, ignoring phone/in-person/email

## Guidelines

- Always include opening and closing rituals
- Balance divergent and convergent activities
- Plan for 20% less than available time (buffer)
- Assign clear roles: facilitator, timekeeper, note-taker
- Test technology before remote sessions
- Create psychological safety before generative activities
