# Company-layer baselines

`/knowledge-setup` proposes one of these as a starting point. All are **fully editable** —
a direction, not a mandate.

## Enterprise — multi-unit, regulated, governance-heavy

- `00-governance/` — ownership, RACI, taxonomy, review/approval workflow
- `01-org/` — operating model, business units, decision rights
- `02-brand/` — brand governance + asset library (DAM), sub-brand rules
- `03-voice/` — messaging architecture, tone by audience, glossary
- `04-legal-compliance/` — regulatory obligations, privacy/data handling, records retention
- `05-security/` — infosec policy, data classification, incident response
- `06-people/` — HR policy, roles/competencies, onboarding, code of conduct
- `07-procurement/` — sourcing rules, approved vendors, third-party risk
- `08-products/` — per-offering dossiers, roadmaps, SLAs
- `09-gtm/` — segments, positioning, pricing
- `10-standards/` — accessibility (WCAG), localization, design system, engineering
- `11-methods/` — playbooks, frameworks
- `12-reference/` — canonical facts, definitions

## SMB / small firm — one entity, light governance

- `00-basics/` — how this repo works, ownership, review norm
- `01-company/` — who we are, team, operating rhythm
- `02-brand-voice/` — visual identity + tone/glossary
- `03-offerings/` — products/services, pricing
- `04-gtm/` — positioning, market, sales/marketing patterns
- `05-methods/` — playbooks and reusable patterns
- `06-policies/` — light legal/security/people essentials
- `07-reference/` — facts, definitions, links

## Solo / small team — a company of one, low ceremony

- `00-basics/` — how this repo works
- `01-offerings/` — what you make / sell
- `02-methods/` — your playbooks & patterns
- `03-stack/` — tools & capabilities
- `04-reference/` — canonical facts, links

> The **personal** layer is separate and universal — see [`personal-layer.md`](personal-layer.md).
> It is scaffolded regardless of company size, into your personal tier.
