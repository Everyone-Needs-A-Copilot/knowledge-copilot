# How I process information

> **Captures** how you *think* — the lenses, frameworks, and first questions you run things
> through, and how you want information handed back to you.
> **Your copilot uses it to** structure research and analysis the way you would, lead with what
> you'd read first, and flag what you'd distrust.

## Fill in

- The first questions you ask of anything new?
- Frameworks / mental models you reach for?
- Conclusion-first or detail-first — how do you want things ordered?
- What makes a source or claim credible to you (and what makes you suspicious)?
- How do you like things *summarized* — what shape?
- Biases you want actively checked?

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- First questions: "what would have to be true?", "what's the reversible version?".
- Models I reach for: second-order effects, opportunity cost, base rates.
- Conclusion first, then the evidence, then what I'd verify.
- Distrust vendor benchmarks and round numbers; trust primary sources and dissent.
- Summarize as: **claim → so-what → what I'd check**.
- Check me for: recency bias, over-indexing on the last thing I read.
