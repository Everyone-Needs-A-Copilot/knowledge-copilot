# Recurring work & automation

> **Captures** the things you do *every X* — and which of them are worth automating.
> **Your copilot uses it to** run, draft, or scaffold each one — and it's the backlog it mines to
> propose promotions from **manual → assisted → automated**. Roughly 3–5 recurring chores drive
> most of the busywork, so you don't need many.

## Fill in

- What do you do every **day / week / month / quarter**?
- For each: what **triggers** it, and what's the finished output?
- Same steps every time, or judgment-heavy?
- How long does it take, and how often does it break?
- Which ones do you **dread**?
- Current state of each: manual · copilot-assisted · automated?

## Is this an automation candidate?

Promote to **automated** when it's mostly YES:

- [ ] **Frequent** (daily/weekly, not once)
- [ ] **Rule-based** (same steps, few branches)
- [ ] **Low-judgment** (little taste/relationship nuance; a wrong output isn't costly)
- [ ] **Stable inputs** (data lives somewhere machine-readable)
- [ ] **Clear trigger** (a time or event, not vibes)
- [ ] **Dull or error-prone** (you hate it / fumble it)
- [ ] **Reversible** (a bad run is cheap to catch and undo)

**Keep human** (→ *copilot-assisted*, draft + you approve) if it's judgment- or
relationship-heavy, inputs shift every time, it's rare, or a mistake is expensive.

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- *Weekly team digest* — trigger: Fri 9am. Collect updates → summarize → post. ~30 min. Same
  every time. → **copilot-assisted** now; full-auto once updates come from a machine-readable source.
- *Monthly invoice reconcile* — judgment-light, stable inputs. → good automation candidate.
- *Quarterly strategy memo* — judgment-heavy. → keep human; copilot drafts, I rewrite.
