# Durable facts — the "don't forget"

> **Captures** stable facts that stay true across sessions — the standing context you're tired of
> repeating.
> **Your copilot uses it to** stop re-asking and stop making you restate the obvious.
> **Recurring context only** — no one-off details, and no secrets (those live in your keychain /
> managed store).

## Fill in

- Standing constraints or commitments?
- Recurring dates / cadences (standups, reviews, releases)?
- Defaults you state repeatedly?
- Things you *always* forget and wish something would remember for you?

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- Releases ship from `main`; feature work on branches.
- Standup Mon 9am; sprint review every other Thu.
- Default DB is Postgres; default package manager is `uv`.
- I always forget to bump the changelog and to tag the release.
