# How I communicate

> **Captures** your voice, tone, formatting defaults, and channel norms.
> **Your copilot uses it to** draft email / chat / docs / PRs in your register without you
> re-briefing it every time — the single highest-leverage thing you can write here.
> Keep it short, first-person, one screen.

## Fill in

- Default tone, and how it shifts by audience (team vs client vs public)?
- Words / phrases you avoid, and ones you overuse (so it can catch them)?
- Preferred length and structure — bullets vs prose, TL;DR up top?
- How direct vs. hedged are you?
- Channel norms — email vs. chat vs. PR vs. doc?
- How you open and sign off?

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- Direct, low-hedge; warmer with clients, terse with the team.
- Avoid: "just", "very", hype adjectives, exclamation points. Overuse: "net".
- Bullets over prose. One ask up top. TL;DR for anything over a screen.
- Email: short, the ask in line 1. PR: *what changed / why / risk*. Chat: lowercase, fast.
- Sign off with a next step, not "thanks!".
