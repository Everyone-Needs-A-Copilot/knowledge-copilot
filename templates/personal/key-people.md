# Key people

> **Captures** the professional relationships you work with regularly and how each likes to work.
> **Your copilot uses it to** tailor communication per recipient and respect who decides what.
> **Professional only** — roles, working preferences, decision rights. No private personal detail.

## Fill in

- Your regular collaborators and their roles?
- Each person's communication preference (async/sync, brief/detailed, channel)?
- Who decides or approves what?
- Any working sensitivities to honor?

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- *Design lead* — owns UX approval. Prefers async + written mockup links. Dislikes surprise meetings.
- *Eng manager* — approves architecture changes. Wants a one-paragraph "why" before a PR.
- *Finance* — signs off on spend > $X. Batches replies; give lead time.
