# Role & expertise

> **Captures** what you know deeply, what you know just enough, what you're learning, and what
> you hand off.
> **Your copilot uses it to** calibrate depth — no over-explaining your strengths, more scaffolding
> in your weak spots — and to route what you'd rather delegate.

## Fill in

- Where do you have **deep** expertise (assume no hand-holding)?
- Where do you have **working** knowledge (explain a bit more)?
- What are you **actively learning**?
- What do you **delegate** or want off your plate?

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- Deep: product strategy, backend/API design, data modeling.
- Working: frontend, infra.
- Learning: applied ML, prompt design.
- Delegate: pixel-level UI polish, copy editing, scheduling.
