# Working preferences

> **Captures** your rhythms, focus/meeting patterns, and feedback style.
> **Your copilot uses it to** schedule, batch, and pace work to fit you — and to give and take
> feedback the way you like.

## Fill in

- Peak-focus hours — when is deep work best / worst?
- Meeting vs. deep-work norms — what do you protect?
- How you like feedback — direct, specific, written, in-the-moment?
- What derails you (and what you want the copilot to guard against)?
- Sync vs. async default?

## Example (illustrative — replace with yours)

- Deep work in the morning; no meetings before 11.
- Async by default; a call only when a thread has looped twice.
- Feedback: direct and specific, no compliment sandwich; written so I can revisit it.
- Derailers: context-switching, unbounded Slack. Batch my notifications; hold non-urgent asks.
- Default to a written summary I can act on later, not a live meeting.
