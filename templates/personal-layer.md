# Personal layer — your professional second brain

The individual layer. It's your **memory and operating manual**, so every copilot (chat, CLI,
coding) acts on *how you actually work* and helps you get through the day — and surfaces what's
worth automating.

**What it is / isn't.** Durable **professional operating knowledge**: how you communicate, how
you look at information, what you work on. **Not** a diary, an autobiography, or a place for
secrets. It lives in your **personal tier** (`<C>-copilot-private`), never in the company layer.

The wizard scaffolds these from [`personal/`](personal/) — each is a short, first-person,
one-screen doc. Templates in `personal/` carry the guiding prompts + a worked example.

| Group | Docs | What the copilot does with them |
|---|---|---|
| **How I operate** | `how-i-communicate` · `how-i-process-information` · `how-i-decide` · `working-preferences` | Drafts, analyzes, decides, and paces work like you would |
| **What I work on** | `what-i-work-on` · `recurring-and-automation` | Grounds every task in live context; surfaces automation candidates |
| **Memory & people** | `durable-facts` · `key-people` | Stops re-asking; tailors comms per person |
| **Competence & tools** | `role-and-expertise` · `tools-and-stack` | Calibrates depth; writes in your stack unprompted |

## Fill order (don't do it all at once)

1. **Start with the three anchors** — `how-i-communicate`, `how-i-process-information`,
   `what-i-work-on` — plus `durable-facts`. A paragraph each beats a blank page.
2. Add the rest as you notice yourself re-briefing the copilot on the same thing twice.

## Keep it living (let the copilot maintain it)

- Bullets, not prose. If a doc tops one screen, you're over-managing it.
- Different half-lives: `what-i-work-on` is the only one you touch weekly (a 5-min review);
  the rest change rarely.
- End of week: *"update my `what-i-work-on` from this week; flag anything I did 3+ times for
  `recurring-and-automation`."* The copilot proposes promotions from **manual → assisted →
  automated** — you approve. That one-way ratchet is how the day gets lighter over time.
