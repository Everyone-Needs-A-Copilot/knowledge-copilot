# 02-products — your product ecosystem

Per-product dossiers, organized by a 3-layer taxonomy. The registry of what exists lives in
`ECOSYSTEM.md` (created by the wizard); the docs live here.

- `01-foundational/` — tools used to *make* things
- `02-work/` — tools used to *do* the work
- `03-applications/` — client-facing products
- `99-innovation-roadmap/` — future ideation, kept separate by design
- `_dossier-template/` — copy this folder per product

**Adding a product:** copy `_dossier-template/` → `<layer>/<product-name>/`, fill it in, and
register the product in `ECOSYSTEM.md`. Keep the registry and the dossier in lockstep.
